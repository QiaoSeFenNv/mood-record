# 今天与主导航

Mode: Operate. Audience: a person recording an emotion in a brief private moment. Task: preview a continuous mood value, explicitly confirm one record, then see its trace, anonymous resonance and time. Scope: today, review, and me share one visual world. Constraints: uni-app / WeChat Mini Program, H5 preview, reduced motion, private data, no invented resonance count. Required illustrations and prompts: `asset-prompts.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A daylight meadow holds the user's changing mood plant and an unjudging animal companion. It refuses the old dark glowing card stack and the reference image's promotional phone collage.

OWN-WORLD: Clear blue-lilac sky and soft green ground make one open scene; coral and warm gold mark traces without ranking them. Original matte storybook raster layers provide the character. Controls use quiet pale surfaces, dark green type, and simple dividers instead of nested cards.

STORY: The user sees today's accumulated traces, explores a mood without saving, confirms deliberately, then sees the new trace and true time. Review explains patterns; Me protects control of data and local appearance.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact date line and record count lead into a broad unframed plant scene with a small companion. The lower portion shows the exact torque and named mood, a full-width continuous slider, then the prominent “记下这一刻” confirmation control. The time line begins below; the next section is visible rather than hidden behind a decorative hero.

FORM: User-pinned reference direction, overriding the `44370704` direction roll after its alternatives were rejected. Signature interaction: dragging paints only a temporary mood trace; a separate confirmation sets it in place, and undo removes precisely that trace. Motion is slow cloud/leaf breathing only when allowed; still frames preserve all information.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
